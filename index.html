About Us

Rooted in craft, made for everyday grace

Isai Fashions began as a small, personal love for Kalamkari prints and handloom cotton — fabrics that feel as good as they look. What started as a few pieces shared with friends has grown into a collection of sarees, kurta sets and festive 3-piece sets, each one chosen or crafted with the same care.

We work closely with small weavers and printers to bring you pieces that carry real craftsmanship, priced fairly, and shipped straight to your door anywhere in India.
